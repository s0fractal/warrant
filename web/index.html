A RECORD, WITH ITS CONTEXT

            
Keep the “why” with the decision.

          

          

            The format travels as plain files. The verifier checks the record
            you have, on your machine.
          

        

        

          

            01 / RECORD
            
What happened?

            

              An actor records a propose, accept,
              reject or supersede decision. Its
              subject, cited policy, reasons and prior records stay together.
            

          

          

            02 / IDENTITY
            
Which exact bytes?

            

              The canonical body has a SHA-256 identity. Ed25519 signatures
              cover a protocol-specific message containing that ID. Referenced
              blobs have their own content addresses.
            

          

          

            03 / RE-RUN
            
Does the reason repeat?

            

              A ski@v1 check runs again against the supplied blobs.
              A prose reason remains prose; cmd@v1 is not executed
              by this browser.
            

          

        

      

      

        

          
READ THE RESULT PRECISELY

          
“No errors” has a meaning.
It also has a boundary.

        

        

          

            ok: true means the requested verification reported zero
            errors. Warnings still matter. An unbound key can verify
            cryptographically while remaining untrusted.
          

          

            ATP limits priced computation; it does not bound all browser memory
            or elapsed time. This demo also limits input size, uses a separate
            worker, and stops unfinished work after 12 seconds. A stopped run is
            incomplete, never a passing report.
          

          

            Changing a record and signing it yourself creates your own record.
            Warrant does not decide whether its facts are true or its policy is
            good.
