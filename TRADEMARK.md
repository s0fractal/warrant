"Warrant" and `warrant-graph` may describe an implementation that follows docs/V3.md and passes this
repository's tests of the parts it claims. An implementation that diverges from docs/V3.md (other
canonical bytes, signature domain, record envelope or assessment rules) takes its own name and states
the commit it forked from. Statements of fact ("reads Warrant records", "based on Warrant") need no
permission.

No trademark is registered; this is a published usage policy.

free of copyleft, transitively.
