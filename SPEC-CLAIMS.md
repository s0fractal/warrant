## Limits the claims record

- SPEC-4.1-2: "condition met, required acceptances" is claimed by SPEC-4.2-2 to SPEC-4.2-10
- SPEC-4.1-10: `serves` (G3) has a witness case and no mutant of its own
- SPEC-4.2-10: "strictly": two items never share an admission position (positions are (at, id) of distinct records), so equality has no case
- SPEC-4.2-13: "under the same rules" (conditions 1-5 for impossible) is witnessed only through the attested and computed cases cited
- SPEC-4.3-5: a supersession rewriting a disposition has a witness (R11) and no mutant of its own
- SPEC-4.4-3: a revision basis through a `subject_changed` trigger that evaluated NOT_FIRED has no witness case cited here
- SPEC-4.4-14: the `compute` half has no case: no witness files a counter-observation against an admitted compute closure
- SPEC-4.5-1: "its `delegable` within both": a grant's record shape puts its `delegable` within its own acts (SPEC §3.5), so a child whose acts are within its parent's `delegable` has its `delegable` within both; no valid record separates the clause, so it has no case, and the engine's check of it has no mutant a valid record could kill
- SPEC-4.6-1: "never inferred from records": no record type carries a profile (SPEC §3), so no case can file one; I1 witnesses only that the profile is the recipient's input and part of the assessment identity
- SPEC-4.7-2: "as of their time" (R21: A relied on as of its own time) has no mutant of its own: a view leaves out records after its as_of, so no mutation of supersession reaches it
- SPEC-4.7-3: "and present" (R21: a view without C) has no mutant of its own: an absent record is never read
- SPEC-5-3: "repository-relative": a path out of the repository is refused by the selector check and, without it, by git, which finds no such path in the tree, so the selector check has no mutant of its own; "no globs": a glob is not refused by name, it is looked up literally (git ls-tree) and finds no file, so it is UNEVALUABLE as a missing path, with no mutant of its own; a submodule is refused by the mode check and again when its blob cannot be read
- SPEC-6-3: one form of competence weighting is burned (a filer's challenged or overclaimed record on other duties); the forms an implementation could invent are not enumerable
- SPEC-6-5: "access to a key" is witnessed (U1: a stranger with a bound key cannot release); generated claims and green CI have no record of their own and no case: a claim is a transition's `established`, which no rule of SPEC §4 reads as authority
